Choose the **suit** first, then the **card**. Work down each table and stop at
the first rule that fits. The ticks show whether a rule applies against
**notrump** or against a **suit** contract. Most rules apply to both.

## Choose the Suit

Against notrump with a weak hand and a weak suit, look for partner’s suit instead.
